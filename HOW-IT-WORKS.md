# How It Works

1. A customer fills out a quote request form (name, contact info, ZIP code, home size, type of cleaning, preferred date).
2. The request lands in the owner's Google Sheet automatically.
3. The system checks whether the ZIP code is inside the service area.
4. It drafts a reply automatically using the owner's own prices and templates (a Pricing tab and an FAQ tab in the sheet).
5. The owner reviews the draft and marks it Approved. Nothing is sent without approval.
6. Approved replies are sent from the business's Gmail (the system checks every 15 minutes).
7. If the customer doesn't respond, it sends up to 2 polite follow-ups (daily check at 9 AM).
8. Every Monday at 8 AM the owner gets a weekly report: new leads, replies sent, follow-ups, and status.
