# Safety and Honesty Rules

- Human approval required before any reply is sent.
- Drafts never promise availability or offer discounts on their own; prices stay within the owner's set ranges.
- Out-of-area leads are flagged, not quoted.
- Email only. No cold texts or calls.
- All outreach follows CAN-SPAM (mailing address and opt-out in every email).
- No fake reviews, testimonials, or made-up results.
